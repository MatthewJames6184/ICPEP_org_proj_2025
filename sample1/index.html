What is ICPEP.SE?

ICpEP.SE - BulSU empowers future computer engineers by bridging the academy and industry,
fostering excellence, unity, and global readiness.

MISSION

The Institute of Computer Engineers of the Philippines Student Edition - Bulacan State University (ICpEP.SE - BulSU) is committed in bridging the gap between the industry and the academe by empowering the interests, welfare, and ideals of its members, developing a strong and harmonious foundation within its community, and professionalizing the skills of its officers and members for global competitiveness and national progression.

VISION

The Institute of Computer Engineers of the Philippines Student Edition - Bulacan State University (ICpEP.SE - BulSU) envisions itself as the foundation of world class computer engineers and motivator of students’ interests towards excellence in Computer Engineering as their field of specialization.

Our Objectives

The fostering and improvement of Computer Engineering education during the new normal setup in the Bulacan State University.

The promulgation and development of the fellowship among Computer Engineering students in the University.

The advancement of knowledge about the latest developments in the Computer Engineering profession.

The enhancement and improvement of Computer Engineering standards, curricula and facilities in the university which include laboratory apparatus and equipment.

The establishment of common interest among Computer Engineering students towards attainment of a successful engineering profession.

The development of good student-instructor relationship.

The encouragement of the Computer Engineering students to collaborate in fellowship and dedication of Science and Technology.

The promotion of association of Computer Engineering students with fellow students within and outside the university throughout the country.

about ICPEP.SE

The Institute of Computer Engineers of the Philippines Student Edition - Bulacan State University (ICpEP.SE - BulSU) is a student organization dedicated to empowering future computer engineers through academic excellence, industry exposure, and leadership development. By bridging the gap between the academe and the tech industry, the organization fosters unity, innovation, and professionalism among its members, preparing them to become globally competitive and socially responsible engineers.
